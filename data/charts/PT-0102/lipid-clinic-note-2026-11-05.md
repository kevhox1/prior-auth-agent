SUBJECTIVE
Seen with a Portuguese interpreter. No chest pain, no claudication. Her father had a heart attack at 48. Taking ezetimibe daily.

EXAM
Bilateral corneal arcus. No tendon xanthomas.

DATA
Untreated LDL-C 236 mg/dL (2019, outside records not on file). LDL-C 188 mg/dL on 10/21/2026 on ezetimibe 10 mg daily (since 03/2022). TSH normal.
Dutch Lipid Clinic Network score 8: LDL-C 190-249 (3), premature CAD in a first-degree relative (1), corneal arcus before age 45 (4). Probable FH. Genetic testing not done.

ASSESSMENT
Heterozygous familial hypercholesterolemia without known ASCVD. Statin intolerant (myalgias on simvastatin). LDL-C far above goal on ezetimibe alone.

PLAN
1. Start evolocumab 140 mg subcutaneously every 2 weeks.
2. Continue ezetimibe 10 mg daily.
3. Offer cascade screening to her two adult children.
