VITALS
BP 132/80. HR 70. Weight 84.0 kg.

SUBJECTIVE
No new neurologic symptoms since the 2025 stroke. Taking rosuvastatin and ezetimibe daily without side effects.

DATA
Lipid panel 10/15/2026: LDL-C 64 mg/dL on rosuvastatin 40 mg and ezetimibe 10 mg. LDL-C at the stroke admission was 152 mg/dL off therapy.

ASSESSMENT
Clinical ASCVD: ischemic stroke from large-artery atherosclerosis, status post carotid endarterectomy, with diabetes. Very high risk. LDL-C 64 mg/dL is above my target of below 55 mg/dL.

PLAN
1. Add evolocumab 140 mg subcutaneously every 2 weeks to reach LDL-C below 55 mg/dL. Continue rosuvastatin 40 mg and ezetimibe 10 mg.
2. Repeat lipid panel 8 to 12 weeks after starting.
