SUBJECTIVE
46-year-old man with ileal Crohn's disease, transferring care after relocation. Reports 4-6 loose stools a day, RLQ cramping after meals, fatigue. He reports he was on adalimumab injections for about 18 months that "worked at first, then stopped working" and his prior GI stopped them in mid-2025. On budesonide now.

ASSESSMENT
Ileal Crohn's disease, symptomatically active on budesonide. Reported prior steroid dependence, methotrexate intolerance, and adalimumab loss of response.

PLAN
- Request outside records from Lakeshore Gastroenterology.
- Fecal calprotectin, CRP.
- Taper budesonide to off over 8 weeks; budesonide is not a maintenance therapy.
- Return in 3 months; colonoscopy if symptoms persist.
