VITALS
Temp 37.0 C. HR 86. BP 112/70.

SUBJECTIVE
Elena reports 8-10 bloody stools a day with urgency for 5 weeks despite infliximab 10 mg/kg every 6 weeks. Last infusion 07/08/2026.

DATA
Infliximab trough (07/08/2026, pre-infusion): <1.0 mcg/mL (undetectable). Antibodies to infliximab: 46 U/mL (reference <10; positive).
Hemoglobin 11.6, CRP 22 (07/22/2026).

ASSESSMENT
Ulcerative pancolitis, severe flare, with secondary loss of response to infliximab (immunogenic). Afebrile, heart rate under 90, hemoglobin above 10.5: does not meet criteria for acute severe ulcerative colitis. Appropriate for outpatient management; return precautions reviewed.

PLAN
- Stop infliximab.
- Prednisone 40 mg daily with taper.
- Flexible sigmoidoscopy to assess activity and exclude CMV.
- Pre-biologic labs before next advanced therapy.
