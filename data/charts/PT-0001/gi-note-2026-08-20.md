SUBJECTIVE
Symptoms first recurred 10/2025 on every-2-week dosing; escalated to adalimumab 40 mg weekly 11/2025 with partial improvement for about 3 months. Progressive symptoms since 04/2026: 6-7 stools a day, nocturnal stooling, RLQ pain, 3 kg weight loss.

DATA
Adalimumab trough (07/30/2026): 1.8 mcg/mL. Anti-adalimumab antibodies: 38 U/mL (reference <10; positive).

ASSESSMENT
Crohn's disease with secondary loss of response to adalimumab, immunogenic, despite dose escalation.

PLAN
- Discontinue adalimumab today.
- Budesonide 9 mg daily as a bridge.
- Colonoscopy to restage.
- Pre-biologic labs: QuantiFERON, hepatitis B panel, CBC, CMP, CRP, fecal calprotectin.
- Plan to switch out of class to ustekinumab pending insurance.
