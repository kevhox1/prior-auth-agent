SUBJECTIVE
Daniel returns to discuss colonoscopy results. Still having 4-5 loose stools a day and periumbilical pain despite azathioprine 150 mg daily for 12 months. Thiopurine metabolites 11/2023: 6-TGN 310 pmol/8x10^8 RBC (therapeutic 235-450), 6-MMP 1,850 pmol/8x10^8 RBC (<5,700). No bleeding.

ASSESSMENT
Crohn's disease, ileocolonic, moderately active, with inadequate response to azathioprine at a therapeutic level. Previously steroid dependent.

PLAN
- Stop azathioprine.
- Start adalimumab 160 mg, then 80 mg at week 2, then 40 mg every 2 weeks.
- Repeat labs in 3 months.
