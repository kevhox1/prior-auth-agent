PROCEDURE: Colonoscopy with ileal intubation and biopsies
INDICATION: Crohn's disease, restaging after loss of response to adalimumab
PREP: Good (Boston Bowel Prep Scale 8)

FINDINGS
- Terminal ileum: multiple large ulcers (>2 cm) over 15 cm, with surrounding erythema and edema.

IMPRESSION
Active ileocolonic Crohn's disease with large ileal ulcers. Endoscopically moderate to severe.
