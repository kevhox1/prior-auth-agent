VITALS
Weight 78.0 kg. BP 118/74. HR 82.

SUBJECTIVE
Somewhat better on budesonide bridge but still 4-5 stools a day with RLQ pain. No fevers. Off adalimumab since 08/20.

DATA
Colonoscopy 09/15/2026: large ileal ulcers, SES-CD 14. Labs 09/22/2026: CRP 24.1, fecal calprotectin 820, QuantiFERON negative, HBsAg and anti-HBc nonreactive.
Harvey-Bradshaw Index today: 11.

ASSESSMENT
Moderately to severely active ileocolonic Crohn's disease. Steroid dependent, inadequate response to azathioprine, secondary loss of response to adalimumab with antibodies.

PLAN
- Start ustekinumab-aekn (preferred biosimilar): 390 mg IV once (weight 78 kg, >55 to 85 kg tier), then 90 mg subcutaneously every 8 weeks starting 8 weeks after the IV dose.
- Submit prior authorization to Northfield.
- Taper budesonide after induction.
