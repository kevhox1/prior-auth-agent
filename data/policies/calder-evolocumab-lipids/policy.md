SECTION A. OVERVIEW

Evolocumab is a PCSK9 inhibitor that lowers low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-C). Calder Mutual covers it for adult members with established atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease or heterozygous familial hypercholesterolemia whose LDL-C stays above goal despite statin and ezetimibe therapy, or who cannot take a statin, when the conditions in Section B are documented.

Benefit. Evolocumab is self-injected under the skin and is dispensed by a network pharmacy. It is reviewed under the member's PHARMACY benefit only. It is not covered under the medical benefit.

SECTION B. MEDICAL NECESSITY CRITERIA FOR INITIAL APPROVAL

Initial approval requires every one of items B.1 through B.8.

B.1 The member has one of the following, as documented by the prescriber in the clinical record:
  - clinical atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (ASCVD): a prior myocardial infarction or acute coronary syndrome, coronary or other arterial revascularization, stable or unstable angina with documented coronary artery disease, ischemic stroke or transient ischemic attack of atherosclerotic origin, or symptomatic peripheral artery disease; or
  - heterozygous familial hypercholesterolemia (HeFH), confirmed by genetic testing or by a Dutch Lipid Clinic Network score of 6 or higher.

B.2 An LDL-C result drawn in the 3 months before the request, while the member was on the lipid-lowering therapy described in B.3 and B.4, is at or above the member's threshold:
  - 70 mg/dL for a member with clinical ASCVD;
  - 100 mg/dL for a member with HeFH and no clinical ASCVD.
  An LDL-C drawn before the current lipid-lowering regimen was started does not meet this requirement.

B.3 The member is on maximally tolerated statin therapy, shown by either (a) or (b). One is enough:
  (a) the member takes a high-intensity statin (atorvastatin 40 to 80 mg daily or rosuvastatin 20 to 40 mg daily), or the highest statin dose the member tolerates when a higher dose caused a documented adverse effect, and has taken it for at least 8 weeks before the LDL-C in B.2, and will continue it with evolocumab; or
  (b) the member cannot take a statin, because of either (i) intolerance of at least two different statins, at least one of them at the lowest approved starting dose, or (ii) a contraindication: a history of statin-associated rhabdomyolysis, or active liver disease.
  Intolerance must be described for each statin: the drug, the dose, the symptom or adverse effect, and that it improved after the statin was stopped or reduced. A statement such as "statin intolerant" without these details does not meet this requirement.

B.4 The member has taken ezetimibe 10 mg daily for at least 4 weeks, now or in the past, with LDL-C remaining at or above the B.2 threshold, or the record documents intolerance of or a contraindication to ezetimibe.

B.5 The prescriber is a cardiologist, an endocrinologist, or a lipid specialist, or the prescriber has consulted with one about this treatment.

B.6 The member is at least 18 years old on the date of the request.

B.7 Evolocumab will not be combined with another PCSK9-directed therapy (alirocumab or inclisiran). When the member is switching from one of these, the record shows the earlier agent is stopping.

B.8 The requested regimen is a labeled adult regimen: 140 mg under the skin every 2 weeks, or 420 mg under the skin once a month.

SECTION C. APPROVAL PERIOD

Initial approvals are issued for 12 months.

SECTION D. RENEWAL

Renewal requires an LDL-C result drawn after starting evolocumab that shows a reduction from the B.2 value, and prescriber attestation that the member continues statin therapy as described in B.3. Renewals are issued for 12 months.

SECTION E. URGENT REQUESTS

A prescriber who attests that the standard review time could seriously jeopardize the member's health may ask for urgent review. Urgent requests are decided within 24 hours of receiving all required information; standard requests within 72 hours.
